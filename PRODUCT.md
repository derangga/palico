# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday, mostly non-technical people who need to convert or resize an image
quickly and don't want to upload personal files to an unknown server. They
arrive with a single concrete job ("turn this HEIC into a JPG", "shrink this
photo for the form") and want it done in a few clicks, on their own device,
often without a reliable connection. They are privacy-conscious and wary of
the typical free converter site.

## Product Purpose

palico is an offline-first image toolkit that runs entirely in the browser
(web workers, no server round-trip). Today it offers an image **converter**
and an image **resizer**, framed as "Your Daily Offline Toolkit." Files never
leave the device. Success means a user completes their task faster than the
ad-laden alternatives, trusts that nothing was uploaded, and comes back the
next time they need a quick image fix.

## Brand Personality

Warm, friendly, and characterful — a helpful daily companion, not a piece of
enterprise software. Three words: **warm, trustworthy, effortless**. The warm
cream palette and a single, friendly typographic voice (Nunito, leaning on
weight and scale for emphasis) are part of the identity and should be
preserved. The personality is approachable without being childish, and
confident without being cold.

## Anti-references

- **Ad-heavy converter sites** (online-convert.com and kin): banner ads,
  upload-to-server flows, popups, fake/disguised download buttons, dark
  patterns. palico is the trustworthy opposite.
- **Cold enterprise SaaS**: generic dashboard chrome, navy-and-gray, soulless
  density. palico has warmth and personality.
- **Over-animated / flashy**: gratuitous motion, gradient-everything,
  attention-grabbing effects. Motion here conveys state, not spectacle.

## Design Principles

- **Privacy is visible, not just claimed.** The offline/on-device guarantee is
  a first-class part of the interface, reinforced where it matters (not buried
  in fine print).
- **One job, few clicks.** Each tool gets the user from input to result with
  minimal decisions. Defaults are sensible; advanced options stay out of the
  way until wanted.
- **Warmth is the differentiator.** In a category of cold, sketchy utilities,
  approachable warmth and character are the competitive edge — preserve them.
- **The tool disappears into the task.** Earned familiarity over novelty;
  standard affordances for standard actions, personality reserved for moments.
- **Trustworthy by feel.** No dark patterns, no fake buttons, no ambiguity
  about what happens to a file. Every interaction should reinforce that this is
  safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch the muted-on-cream
combinations), visible keyboard focus states, full keyboard operability for
file selection and all controls, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative
for every animation. Drag-and-drop must have a keyboard/click-accessible
equivalent.
